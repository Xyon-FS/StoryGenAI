# Origins and research

[← Project overview](../README.md)

StoryGEN AI builds on research into AI-assisted narrative design and human–AI co-creation. Its approach combines structured generation with author review: the author shapes the creative direction, inspects results and decides which versions to approve.

The current web app develops this approach into a practical workspace for authors and game designers, with a staged workflow, editable results and local project storage.

## Research foundation

The project originated in Federico Maglione's master's thesis on generative AI and narrative design. The thesis documents the research foundation and the earlier system; it should not be read as a specification of every feature in the current web app.

[Read the master's thesis (PDF) →](https://federicomaglione.com/file/MasterThesis/Tesi_StoryTeller_GenAI.pdf)

## Narrative example

**ARIA: The Architect Protocol** is a narrative example associated with the research. It provides additional context for the approach to story development.

[Read ARIA: The Architect Protocol (PDF) →](https://federicomaglione.com/file/MasterThesis/ARIA%20The%20Architect%20Protocol.pdf)
